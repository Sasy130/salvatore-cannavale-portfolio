## Responsive delivery

`public/optimized/` contains locally resized WebP derivatives of the licensed originals at 320, 480, 768, 1080, 1440 and 1920 pixels. Generated by `scripts/prepare-images.mjs` with Sharp; no synthetic image content is added. The original JPEG hashes and sources remain in `public/asset-manifest.json`. The Next.js image loader serves these responsive derivatives without a server image endpoint.
