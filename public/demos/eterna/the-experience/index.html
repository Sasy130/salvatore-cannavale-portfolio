A LITTLE CLARITY

Before we
begin.

Can I book a real visit through this website?+

No. ÉTERNA is an unofficial demonstration. The contact page previews a request without sending or storing personal information.

Are the spaces and services real?+

The names are configurable concepts. Images show independent properties and events. Capacities, permissions, services and supplier arrangements are not verified for an ÉTERNA venue.

What would I need for a personalised request?+

An occasion, preferred dates, an approximate guest count and the details that matter to you. A real proposal would follow confirmation with the actual venue.

What about accessibility and weather?+

A real venue visit should confirm step-free routes, accessible facilities, seating needs and a suitable indoor alternative. These cannot be guaranteed by this demonstration.
