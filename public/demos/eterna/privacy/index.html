PRIVACY & YOUR REQUEST

09 / THE WORLD OF ÉTERNA

Considered care.
For your details, too.

Your request

This deployment is in demonstration mode. In demonstration mode, the enquiry and tour journeys use your browser memory only: no submission is made, no email is sent and no details are written to browser storage or a database. Use sample information. Reset the journey or close the tab to discard it.

When real requests are enabled

The form records the contact details, event or visit preferences, and notes you choose to provide. These are used to answer your request and discuss a possible event or tour, rather than for marketing. An enquiry does not confirm a date, price or appointment.

Live records are encrypted in the application before database storage. Access is restricted to authorised administrators. Do not include financial account information, identity documents, health details or other sensitive personal information in the notes.

Operator & retention

A real venue operator has not yet been configured. Real collection must not be enabled until the responsible operator and privacy contact are identified. No verified privacy contact is currently published.

The configured retention target is 90 days. A live operator must schedule the documented retention task, review actual legal obligations and backup retention, and provide the applicable rights and complaint information for its jurisdiction. Administrators can delete an individual request.

Technical information

No advertising analytics or marketing cookies are included. The public journeys use no tracking or persistent draft storage. The live admin area uses an essential, HttpOnly session cookie lasting up to eight hours. Rate limiting stores pseudonymous counters; audit records store actions and record identifiers without request content.

Fonts and licensed photographs are self-hosted. An interactive OpenStreetMap, if configured, loads only after you request it and then connects to that provider. This hosted demonstration may require ChatGPT sign-in; hosting access is separate from the venue admin login.

Before going live

This concept information must be reviewed and completed by the actual operator with its legal basis, processors, international transfers, local rights and regulator details before collecting real personal data.
