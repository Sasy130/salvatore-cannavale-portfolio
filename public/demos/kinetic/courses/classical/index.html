Classical
Precision becomes poetry.

A practice to make your own.

Build your foundation. Discover alignment, musicality and the freedom that comes from technique.

LEVEL
Foundation / Intermediate
SAMPLE TIMETABLE
Mon & Wed · 18:00–19:30
FACULTY
Elena Moreau
DURATION / AGE
90 minutes / Adults 18+

Inside the class

A progressive barre warm-up leads into centre practice, balance and travelling combinations. Explore posture, coordination and musical phrasing.

Before you arrive

Wear comfortable clothing that allows you to move freely. Bring water. For classical classes, soft ballet shoes are suggested; contemporary is practised barefoot; urban uses clean indoor trainers.
